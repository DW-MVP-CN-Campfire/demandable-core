## Guidelines

You may:

- Refer to the project by name to describe it accurately (for example, "built on Demandable Core" or "compatible with Demandable").
- Use the name when discussing, reviewing, or teaching about the project.

Please don't:

- Use the name or logos in a way that suggests your fork, product, or service is official or endorsed by the maintainers.
- Use confusingly similar names for products or services, especially anything that handles payments or funds.

Forks that diverge from the project should use their own name. Third-party names mentioned in this repository (such as Arbitrum) belong to their respective owners.

Questions about naming or permission: contact the maintainers at security@demandable.com.
